# letter-garden product

letter-garden helps a player find words from a puzzle's seven letters. The player picks the required center letter and sees which results use all seven distinct letters.

The app runs in the browser, with a single-column layout for phones, native keyboard controls, and the solver suite's light and dark themes. Results are shareable and refreshable.

The dictionary is Debian wamerican, derived from SCOWL. A general dictionary can omit words the game accepts and include words it rejects. The app has no scoring, hints, daily puzzle feed, accounts, or found-word tracking.
